Privacy Policy – Curiosity Byte Bot

Curiosity Byte Bot is a private tool used only by the owner of the Curiosity Byte YouTube channel to upload videos, read channel analytics and store video files in the owner's Google Drive.

It does not collect, sell or share data from any other user. Google account data accessed by the bot is used only to operate the owner's own channel and is not shared with third parties.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
